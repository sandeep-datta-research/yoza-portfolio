HELLO, I’M YOZA

Creating worlds, one little detail at a time.

I’m an independent creative drawn to expressive characters, thoughtful composition and the feeling a good image can leave behind.

My process moves between playful experimentation and careful refinement. I’m inspired by everyday scenes, nature, stories, and the visual language of animation and illustration.

This portfolio is a growing archive of that curiosity — a place for finished pieces, ongoing explorations and collaborations.
